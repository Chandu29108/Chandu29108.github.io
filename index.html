What I actually do

12 projects across classification, deep learning, multi-agent AI, live SaaS products, and analytics — this is the toolkit behind them.

As an AI Engineer Intern at Rubixe (Nov 2025 – Jun 2026), I worked across the full ML lifecycle — data cleaning, EDA, feature engineering, model building, and deployment — on classification, deep learning, and computer vision problems. That work included a churn risk-scoring system for a telecom client and CNN-based disease detection on small image datasets. Since then I've shipped live products of my own: Propensity, a multi-tenant SaaS that predicts which customers will buy again, and NetAgent, a multi-agent LLM system for network diagnostics.

Before that, I completed PwC's Power BI Job Simulation through Forage, building executive dashboards on call center KPIs and diversity metrics. I care about the same thing across all of it: does the output on screen actually help someone make a better decision, whether that output is a churn score, a chart, or an agent's answer.

B.Tech graduate, NIT Durgapur (2021–2025). Open to Data Scientist, ML Engineer, and AI Engineer roles.
